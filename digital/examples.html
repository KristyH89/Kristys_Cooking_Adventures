From drawing to photo

A portrait that starts as a technical drawing. Move your mouse over it and a thin line sweeps across, wiping the drawing away to reveal the photo underneath.

On a phone there is no mouse, so the same thing happens while you scroll: down to see the photo, back up to bring the drawing back.
