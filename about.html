About me

Hi! I'm Kristy.

I grew up in a small village in the Netherlands, but these days you'll find me in Småland, Sweden, where I live with my husband and our two boys.

A few years ago, we decided to make a big change. We were looking for more peace, quiet and space, and Sweden had all three. My oldest sister had already been living here for over twenty years, which made Sweden feel a lot less far away and the step a lot less daunting.

Looking back, I'm incredibly happy with our decision. I love where we live, the contact we have with our neighbours and how quickly we made new friends. I haven't regretted the move for a single second.

Life in the kitchen

One thing you'll definitely notice on this website: I love cooking! Since moving to Sweden, my collection of kitchen gadgets has grown quite a bit, from a food processor to a slow cooker and a bread maker.

I enjoy trying new recipes, experimenting with ingredients and finding new ways to make something delicious. I'm not exactly a sports enthusiast, but I do like going for walks and trying to stay active, which helps balance out all that baking.

Creative in my own way

I've never been the artistic type when it comes to painting or drawing, but creativity comes in many forms. I've always been interested in computers and in finding clever ways to make things easier. Even as a teenager, I was looking for shortcuts. Why copy and paste with a mouse when you can simply press Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V?

When I was around fifteen, I built my own website with HTML and CSS. That interest has only grown since. These days, I'm especially interested in web design, usability, content management systems and all the little details that make a website work well and look good.

My professional background

From social work to web design

After studying Social Work in the Netherlands, I worked for around ten years as a Social Support Advisor (in Dutch: Wmo consultant) for Dutch municipalities, helping residents get support under the Dutch Social Support Act.

I visited people at home to understand their situation and decide whether they were eligible for practical or psychosocial support. It was rewarding work, and I genuinely enjoyed helping people find the support they needed.

At the same time, I felt more and more drawn to the world of IT. While still in the Netherlands, I took a systems administration course with Centric as a first step towards a career in IT.

Then life took a different turn, and we moved to Sweden! My career path took an unexpected detour, but my interest in technology and digital creativity never went away. Right now I'm finishing an internship as a web designer and web developer, and I'm looking for a fun new challenge where I can put that interest to work.

You can read more about my digital interests and projects in Digital Adventures.
